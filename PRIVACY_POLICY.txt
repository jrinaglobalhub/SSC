1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

JRINA Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection

  a) SmartShopper Consumer ("SSC") mobile application;
  b) SmartShopper Merchant ("SSM") mobile application & POS suite; and

By installing, registering with, accessing, or using SmartShopper, you acknowledge

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA WE ACTUALLY COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to provide hyper-local deal
discovery, retail counter billing, and in-app communications.

A. Consumer Account & Profile Information (SSC):
   - Identity & Contact: Full name, verified mobile phone number, and optional
     email address during registration or OTP login.
   - SmartShopper Customer ID: A unique system-generated identifier (e.g.,
     "SS-CUS-xxxx") encoded in a personal QR code displayed at retail checkout
     to facilitate counter loyalty and digital e-bill delivery.
   - Saved Delivery Addresses: House/apartment details, street, city (e.g., Palode),
     district, and pincode provided voluntarily for order fulfillment.

B. Location Data & Retention Architecture:
   - Foreground Consented Location: With your explicit permission, SSC accesses
     your device's GPS coordinates while the app is in use to calculate distances
     to nearby stores and display local promotions.
   - Quick Location Preset: Users may manually select quick locality presets
     (such as Palode, Kerala) without granting hardware GPS permissions.
   - Notification Geofence Storage: To dispatch real-time 5,000-metre proximity
     push alerts when partner stores publish flash deals, SSC stores your latest
     consented coordinate pair (latitude, longitude) and location source
     ("verified_device_gps" or "verified_saved_address") in our secure Supabase
     `user_push_tokens` table.
   - What We DO NOT Collect: SSC does NOT track or store continuous background
     GPS trails, speed, travel routes, or historical breadcrumb trails.

C. Merchant Business Data (SSM):
   - Store trade name, physical store address, GPS coordinates for radar boundary,
     contact telephone, business category, GSTIN (where registered), and bank
     settlement accounts for merchant SaaS transactions.

D. Transactions & In-App Communications:
   - Digital E-Bills: Counter sales invoices generated by partner merchants
     displaying purchased item names, quantities, invoice totals, and GST breakdown.
   - Voucher Redemptions: Status of claimed promotional vouchers, redemption
     timestamps, and single-use verification tokens.
   - Customer-Merchant Chat: Direct inquiry messages exchanged between shoppers
     and store managers via our real-time messaging pipeline.
   - AI Shopping Assistant: Ephemeral conversational text queries processed via
     Google Gemini 1.5 Flash to assist with store product suggestions. Chat queries
     are processed in real time and are not sold to third-party data brokers.

E. Financial & Payment Credentials (EXCLUSIONS):
   - Consumer checkouts are settled directly between consumers and merchants
     via Cash on Delivery (COD) or direct UPI. SSC does not collect, process, or
     store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking PINs.
   - Merchant platform subscription fees on SSM are processed directly by
     RBI-authorized payment gateways (Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We use collected data solely for legitimate, operational purposes:
  1. Hyper-Local Deal Radar: Evaluating merchant deal geofences against your latest
     consented location to dispatch deals within 5,000 metres.
  2. Loyalty Identification: Rendering your Customer ID QR code to enable partner
     stores to credit loyalty points and attach digital receipts.
  3. Push Notifications: Transmitting real-time order status, deal alerts, and
     incoming chat messages via Expo Push Notifications and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  4. Real-Time Chat & Customer Support: Relaying customer inquiries to merchants.
  5. Security & Fraud Prevention: Verifying single-use voucher QR tokens to prevent
     counter fraud or unauthorized coupon reuse.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS & DATA TRANSFERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Personal data is processed only through vetted infrastructure partners under
strict confidentiality and security standards:
  - Supabase Inc.: Cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database, authentication, and Realtime
    engine with TLS 1.3 transit encryption and Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  - Google LLC / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Secure delivery of Android push
    notifications.
  - Expo (650 Industries Inc.): Notification routing infrastructure.
  - Google Cloud Vertex AI / Gemini: Ephemeral processing of conversational queries
    for the in-app AI Shopping Assistant.
  - Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.: Secure payment processing exclusively for
    merchant SaaS platform subscriptions on SSM.
  - OpenStreetMap & Leaflet: Geographic mapping tiles and coordinate distance calculations.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to advertisers or third
parties for behavioral marketing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA SECURITY & RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Security Safeguards: All network communications are encrypted in transit using
  HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Database tables are protected by Supabase Row-Level Security
  (RLS) ensuring consumers and merchants can only access authorized records.
- Retention Period: Account data and digital bills are retained for the active life
  of the user account. Inactive push notification tokens are automatically pruned.
  Statutory tax records (such as merchant GST sales invoices) are retained for the
  mandatory audit period required under Indian financial regulations.

6. USER RIGHTS UNDER DPDP ACT, 2023
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have:
  - Right to Access & Correction: View and edit your profile details and saved
    addresses directly in the Account tab.
  - Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke device location or push notification
    permissions at any time via Android system settings or in-app notification toggles.
  - Right to Erasure / Account Deletion: Request complete deletion of your account
    and associated personal data by contacting grievance@jrina.online or
    support@jrina.online. Eligible records will be permanently purged within 30 days.
  - Right to Nominate & Grievance Redressal: Nominate a representative or submit
    data privacy grievances to our Grievance Desk.
