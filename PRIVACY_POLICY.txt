1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JRINA Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy
and is committed to protecting the personal data of all users of the SmartShopper
ecosystem, including:
  a) SmartShopper Consumer ("SSC") mobile application and web portals.
  b) SmartShopper Merchant ("SSM") mobile application, POS, and merchant tools.
  c) Related online services accessible via https://jrina.online.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and disclose
your information when you download, install, register, access, or use our mobile
applications and related services. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By
accessing or using SSC or SSM, you agree to the collection and use of information
in accordance with this policy and applicable laws, including the Information
Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our hyper-local deal
discovery, retail workstation, and billing services.

A. Information You Provide to Us:
   - Account & Profile Data: Full name, mobile phone number, email address,
     profile picture, and authentication credentials.
   - Merchant Business Information (SSM): Store/Business trade name, physical
     store address, GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number), business
     category, bank account/settlement details for payouts, and store operating hours.
   - Product & Inventory Data (SSM): Product descriptions, barcodes/SKUs, shelf
     pricing, stock levels, and discount deal configurations.
   - Customer Transactions (SSC & SSM): Counter billing entries, digital receipts,
     invoice totals, GST breakdown, voucher redemption codes, and purchase history.
   - Communications: Messages sent through customer-merchant in-app chat, support
     queries, and verified buyer reviews or ratings.

B. Information Collected Automatically:
   - Geolocation Data:
     * SSC: Precise or approximate foreground location to detect nearby partner
       stores, radar flash deals, and distance-based promotions within your
       selected geofence radius (500m - 5000m).
     * SSM: Store GPS coordinates calibration to establish your store's radar
       beacon and geofence boundary.
   - Device & Technical Data: Device model, hardware manufacturer, operating
     system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, connection type,
     app version, crash logs, and performance metrics.
   - Push Notification Tokens: Expo Push Notification tokens and Firebase Cloud
     Messaging (FCM) device tokens required to deliver real-time deal alerts,
     chat messages, and billing updates.

C. Information We DO NOT Collect:
   - We do not store sensitive payment credentials such as credit/debit card
     numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking passwords. All online payments are handled
     directly by RBI-regulated payment gateways (such as Cashfree Payments).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:
  1. Service Delivery: Facilitating counter billing, catalog management, coupon
     issuance, real-time voucher verification, and in-store redemptions.
  2. Location-Based Features: Delivering real-time localized deal notifications to
     shoppers physically within a merchant's designated radar geofence.
  3. Real-Time Communication: Enabling instant customer inquiry chats and merchant
     responses between verified buyers and stores.
  4. Invoice & Compliance: Generating GST-compliant digital invoices and audit
     records for retail merchants.
  5. Security & Fraud Prevention: Verifying single-use voucher authenticity,
     preventing duplicate redemptions, and securing merchant counter sessions.
  6. Service Improvements & Analytics: Monitoring app performance, debugging crashes,
     and improving user interface usability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & SERVICE PROVIDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We share data only with trusted third-party service providers essential for app
functionality, subject to strict confidentiality and security agreements:
  - Supabase Inc.: Cloud database, user authentication, and secure Realtime
    sync infrastructure (Data encrypted in transit and at rest).
  - Expo / Firebase (Google LLC): Push notification infrastructure for dispatching
    background and foreground notifications for deals, chats, and redemptions.
  - Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.: Secure payment processing for merchant
    subscription fees or consumer digital checkouts where applicable.
  - OpenStreetMap / Location Services: Map tiles and geocoding services.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to any third-party
advertisers or data brokers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA STORAGE, SECURITY & RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Security Measures: We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and
  technical security safeguards, including HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit,
  Row-Level Security (RLS) database policies, and authenticated server-side API
  pipelines.
- Data Retention: We retain user and transaction data only as long as necessary
  to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by statutory
  accounting, tax (GST records), and commercial regulations under Indian law.
- Inactive Tokens: Push notification tokens that are invalid, unregistered, or
  superseded by a new device session are automatically pruned or deactivated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA ACCESS, DELETION & USER RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You have the following rights regarding your personal information:
  - Access & Correction: You may review and update your profile information, store
    profile, or catalog directly within the SSC or SSM apps.
  - Permissions Management: You can revoke camera permissions (used for QR code
    scanning) or location permissions at any time via your Android device settings.
    (Note: Disabling location may limit radar deal discovery).
  - Account & Data Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and
    associated personal data by contacting our Grievance Officer at
    support@jrina.online. Upon verification, eligible records will be permanently
    purged within 30 days, except records legally mandated for GST and tax audits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SmartShopper services (both SSC and SSM) are not directed to individuals under
the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If
we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will take prompt
steps to delete such information from our servers.
