# SmartShopper Consumer — Privacy Policy

**Operated by:** JRINA Private Limited  
**Website:** https://ssc.jrina.online  
**Effective Date:** 28 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 28 October 2026  

## 1. Introduction

JRINA Private Limited (“JRINA”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates SmartShopper Consumer (“SSC”), a platform that helps users discover local merchants, explore deals, and access purchasing functionality where available.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use SSC.

## 2. Regional Availability

SSC provides different functionality depending on the user's location.

- **Kerala, India:** Eligible users may access available purchasing functionality, subject to merchant participation, serviceability, and checkout conditions.
- **Outside Kerala:** SSC is limited to merchant and deal discovery. Purchase functionality through SSC is not available in these locations unless JRINA expressly introduces it.

The information processed may vary depending on the features you use.

## 3. Information We May Collect

### 3.1 Account information
Depending on the registration and authentication features you use, SSC may process your name, email address, phone number, account identifier, and authentication-related information.

### 3.2 Location information
SSC may request GPS location permission to identify nearby merchants and display location-relevant deals.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may then become unavailable or less accurate.

Background location collection, continuous tracking, and precise-location storage will not be represented as practices unless verified in the deployed application.

### 3.3 Order and transaction information
Where purchasing functionality is available in Kerala, SSC may process information necessary to support an order, such as order references, merchant details, selected products or services, delivery information, transaction status, and refund requests, to the extent those features are implemented.

Payment credentials should be handled by the applicable payment provider where its integration is used. The precise data flow must be verified against the production implementation.

### 3.4 Technical information
Depending on enabled functionality, technical information may include device and application identifiers, notification tokens, operating-system information, crash reports, and diagnostic events.

### 3.5 Support communications
If you contact us, we may process your contact details and the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, investigate a complaint, or address a refund request.

## 4. How We Use Information

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

- Create and maintain user accounts.
- Display nearby merchants and deals.
- Support purchasing and order fulfilment in eligible locations.
- Coordinate order and delivery-related information.
- Process or investigate transaction and refund requests.
- Provide customer support and grievance handling.
- Maintain application security and reliability.
- Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will not claim that a particular feature collects information unless that collection is supported by the actual implementation.

## 5. Location Permission

Location permission is requested to support location-dependent functionality.

You may disable location access through your device settings. This may affect nearby merchant discovery and related features.

The exact frequency of GPS access, location precision, retention period, and whether coordinates are stored on a server depend on the deployed application and must be verified before publication.

## 6. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with relevant parties when necessary to provide the service, subject to applicable law. These may include:

- Participating merchants involved in an order or customer enquiry.
- Delivery partners, where delivery functionality is used.
- Payment providers handling an eligible transaction.
- Hosting, database, authentication, mapping, and notification providers actually used by SSC.
- Authorities where disclosure is required by law.

The information shared will depend on the transaction and the role of each party. We will not claim that all listed providers are active integrations without verifying the production configuration.

For purchases completed directly with a merchant outside SSC, the merchant's own privacy and payment arrangements may apply.

## 7. Platform Fees and Delivery Information

Where applicable, SSC may process information concerning the platform fee, delivery charge, order status, and related refund requests.

The current fee and refund terms are described in the Refund & Cancellation Policy. The actual amounts and terms applicable to a transaction will be disclosed where required before confirmation.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant service, support, security, accounting, and legal purposes, subject to applicable law.

Retention periods may vary by data category. Information may need to be retained where legally required, including for legitimate transaction records or dispute resolution.

Specific retention and deletion timelines must be confirmed against the actual database, backup, and account-deletion processes.
