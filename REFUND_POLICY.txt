1. INTRODUCTION & PLATFORM ROLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the guidelines, terms, and procedures
governing cancellations, returns, refunds, and financial disputes across the
SmartShopper platform, comprising SmartShopper Consumer ("SSC") and SmartShopper
Merchant ("SSM"), operated by JRINA Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our").

JRINA Private Limited operates SmartShopper strictly as an intermediary technology
and digital discovery platform under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act,
2000. SmartShopper facilitates hyper-local deal discovery, proximity radar alerts,
digital voucher issuance, and customer-merchant direct communication.

JRINA Private Limited is NOT a seller, retailer, distributor, or manufacturer of
goods, groceries, or services listed by independent merchant partners.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONSUMER RETAIL PURCHASES (SSC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1. Transaction Settlement Method:
All retail transactions initiated through or facilitated by SmartShopper Consumer
(including counter purchases, click-and-collect, or merchant delivery orders) are
settled directly between the consumer and the independent merchant partner using:
  a) Cash on Delivery / Counter Cash (COD), or
  b) Direct Peer-to-Merchant Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transfer.

SmartShopper Consumer DOES NOT collect, process, hold, or route consumer purchase
funds through an escrow, wallet, or payment intermediary account.

2.2. Merchant Responsibility & Return Policies:
Each independent merchant partner establishes, maintains, and enforces their own
return, replacement, cancellation, and refund policies in accordance with applicable
consumer protection standards.
  a) Perishable Items (e.g., fresh groceries, vegetables, milk, meat, bakery items):
     Typically non-returnable once accepted at counter delivery, unless demonstrably
     spoiled or defective at the point of receipt.
  b) Non-Perishable Goods: Subject to individual merchant store return windows,
     provided items are returned in original packaging with physical invoices.
  c) Order Cancellations: Consumers may cancel a pending order directly with the
     merchant before dispatch or fulfillment by contacting the merchant via the
     in-app chat or phone number provided on the store profile.

2.3. Processing of Consumer Refunds:
Because consumer purchase payments are made directly to the merchant (via cash or
direct UPI), any approved return or refund must be reimbursed directly by the
respective merchant to the consumer. JRINA Private Limited does not possess consumer
retail funds and cannot execute automated financial chargebacks for retail goods.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DIGITAL VOUCHERS & PROMOTIONAL OFFERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. Promotional Instrument Nature:
Digital vouchers, flash deals, and promotional coupons claimed within the SSC app
are non-monetary, single-use promotional instruments intended to grant discounts
at participating partner stores.

3.2. No Cash Value:
Vouchers possess no independent cash face value, cannot be redeemed for legal tender,
cannot be transferred to third-party bank accounts, and cannot be refunded or exchanged
for currency under any circumstances.

3.3. Merchant Honoring & Expiry:
Vouchers must be redeemed at the merchant counter prior to the stated expiration date
and subject to specific offer criteria (e.g., minimum purchase threshold). Expired,
previously redeemed, or invalidated vouchers cannot be reissued or refunded.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MERCHANT SAAS SUBSCRIPTION CHARGES (SSM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1. Nature of Merchant Charges:
Charges levied by JRINA Private Limited on the SmartShopper Merchant ("SSM")
application represent recurring business-to-business (B2B) software-as-a-service
(SaaS) platform subscription and technology licensing fees, processed through
RBI-authorized payment aggregators (such as Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.).

4.2. Cancellation of Merchant Subscriptions:
Merchants may cancel their platform subscription renewal at any time via the SSM
dashboard or by notifying merchant support at support@jrina.online. Cancellation takes
effect at the conclusion of the currently active paid billing cycle.

4.3. Subscription Refund Eligibility:
  a) Initial Activation Window: A merchant may request a full refund of platform fees
     within seven (7) calendar days of initial account onboarding if the software or
     radar broadcast engine demonstrably fails to activate due to verifiable technical
     defects on JRINA's infrastructure.
  b) Post-Activation Period: Subscription fees are non-refundable once the platform
     tools, store profile, and radar broadcast systems have been operational during
     the billing cycle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. FAILED, DUPLICATE, OR DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1. Technical Duplicate Charges:
In the event of duplicate billing or debits occurring on JRINA's merchant SaaS
subscription gateway, verified duplicate amounts will be reversed to the original
payment source within 5 to 7 business days following merchant notification.

5.2. Consumer-Merchant Dispute Mediation:
While JRINA Private Limited is not a party to retail consumer transactions, we are
committed to maintaining a trustworthy marketplace. If a consumer experiences an
unresolved dispute regarding defective goods, unhonored valid vouchers, or incorrect
billing by a listed partner merchant:
  a) The consumer may submit details (including merchant name, bill photo, date,
     and description of issue) to support@jrina.online.
  b) JRINA will facilitate communication with the merchant to seek an amicable
     resolution. Repeated merchant non-compliance will result in suspension or
     permanent removal from the SmartShopper platform.
