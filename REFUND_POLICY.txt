# SmartShopper Consumer — Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Operated by:** JRINA Private Limited  
**Website:** https://ssc.jrina.online  
**Effective Date:** 28 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 28 October 2026  

## 1. Scope

SmartShopper Consumer (“SSC”) provides merchant and deal discovery outside Kerala and supports purchasing functionality in Kerala where enabled.

This policy explains the ordinary treatment of cancellations, platform fees, delivery charges, and refund requests involving SSC-related transactions.

All refund decisions remain subject to applicable law and mandatory consumer rights.

## 2. Regional Availability

### Kerala
Eligible users may access purchasing functionality where enabled in SSC. The applicable checkout conditions, payment arrangements, delivery service, and refund terms will depend on the transaction.

### Outside Kerala
SSC is limited to deal and merchant discovery. Purchasing functionality through SSC is not available in these locations under the current model.

## 3. Platform Fee — ₹1

Where applicable, a platform fee of ₹1 may be charged for an eligible transaction. The fee must be disclosed before the transaction is confirmed.

The platform fee is ordinarily non-refundable once the relevant platform service has been provided or the transaction has been processed.

However, a refund may be considered or required where the fee was charged incorrectly, charged more than once, the relevant service was not provided, or applicable law otherwise requires a refund.

## 4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, where applicable, will be disclosed before order confirmation.

Delivery charges are ordinarily non-refundable once the delivery service has been completed.

Where delivery has not been performed, an order is cancelled before the service is provided, delivery fails, or another circumstance gives rise to a legally required refund, the request will be assessed based on the facts and applicable law.

This policy does not exclude refunds or other remedies that are legally required.

## 5. Order Cancellation

A cancellation request should be submitted as early as possible through the available order or support channel.

Whether an order can be cancelled depends on its status, whether fulfilment or delivery has begun, the relevant transaction terms, and applicable law.

Any applicable cancellation fee or other deduction must be disclosed where required and must comply with applicable law.

No cancellation request will override a mandatory legal right to a refund or other remedy.

## 6. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are assessed individually, considering:

- The nature and status of the transaction.
- Whether the relevant product or service was provided.
- The reason for cancellation or the refund request.
- Whether a payment was duplicated or incorrectly collected.
- The role of JRINA, the merchant, and the payment provider.
- The applicable transaction terms and mandatory legal requirements.

The platform fee and delivery charges are ordinarily non-refundable as described above, subject to legally required refunds and other applicable consumer rights.

## 7. Refund Processing

Where JRINA is responsible for processing a refund, the applicable process and expected timeline will be communicated according to the transaction terms and payment-provider arrangements.

Where the merchant or an external payment provider is responsible for a transaction, users may need to contact that party.

SSC does not promise a universal refund-processing period. Any legally prescribed deadline takes precedence over an internal review process.

## 9. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts rights or remedies available under applicable Indian law, including remedies for misleading representations, unfair trade practices, deficient services, or other legally recognised grounds.

Where JRINA, a merchant, or another service provider is legally responsible for a refund or other remedy, the applicable obligation remains in force.

## 10. Policy Changes

JRINA may update this policy to reflect changes to SSC, its transaction arrangements, or applicable law. The latest version will be published on the SSC website with its effective or last-updated date.
